Napoli is the only Fan Token pulling money in a red market

FanTokens Newsroom · 10 Sep 2026, 14:20 UTC · 3 min read
NAP
SSC Napoli
$0.2253 · volume $2.67M
+11.4%
24h

Forty-seven of the sixty-five Fan Tokens we track are down today. CHZ is off 5.8%, the fear and greed index reads 69, and the median token in the universe has lost 2.0%. On a day like that, most green numbers are noise.

Napoli's is not. NAP is up 11.4% on $2.67M of volume — against $175,665 in the previous twenty-four hours. That is a 556% increase measured after the market-wide move is stripped out, which is the only way to read a volume spike honestly.

The flow arrives before the price. It did for Sevilla too, and Sevilla has not kicked a ball yet.

The second name on the flow table is stranger. SEVILLA traded $232,426 against $11,067 the day before — a 2,002% jump — and the token is still down 2.4%. Sevilla play Valencia tomorrow at 19:00 UTC. Somebody is positioning; the price has not moved yet.

Napoli's health grade held at B, but its price-stability pillar fell from 90 to 75: the move is large relative to Napoli's own history, which is exactly what a real repricing looks like and exactly what a wash-trading pattern does not.
