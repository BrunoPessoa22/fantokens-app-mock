Fifty-three of the sixty-five Fan Tokens we track are down today. CHZ is off 5.8%, the fear and greed index reads 69, and the median token has lost 2.0%. On a day like that, most green numbers are noise.

Napoli's is not. The rotation table, which compares matched windows, puts $1.15M against $175,665 the day before — a 554% jump, or 556% once the market-wide move is stripped out.

The flow arrives before the price. It did for Sevilla too, and Sevilla has not kicked a ball yet.

The second name is stranger. SEVILLA traded $232,426 against $11,067 the day before — a 2,002% jump — and the token is still down 2.4%. Sevilla play Valencia tomorrow at 19:00 UTC.

Napoli's health grade held at B, but its price-stability pillar fell from 90 to 75: the move is large against Napoli's own history, which is what a real repricing looks like and what a wash-trading pattern does not.
